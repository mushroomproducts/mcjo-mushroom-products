Since 2020


MCJO Mushroom Products is a homegrown mushroom enterprise located at 616 Riverside, Carino, Paniqui, Tarlac, Philippines.


We specialize in transforming fresh oyster mushrooms into nutritious, value-added products that Filipino families can enjoy every day.


Our mission is to provide healthy, affordable, and delicious mushroom products while supporting local agriculture and sustainable food production.
